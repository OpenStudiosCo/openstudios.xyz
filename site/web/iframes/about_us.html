About Us

We are Open Studios, an ethically led multi disciplinary digital studio.

Established in 2019, we craft unique worlds that tell stories.

Specialists in 3D production using custom pipelines and FOSS tools.

We are a husband and husband team, two dogs and a cat.

Paul Brzeski

Paul is a writer, creative technologist and co-founder of Open Studios. With two decades of experience in IT and digital media, he brings storytelling, software and visual experimentation together through games, fiction and interactive experiences.

His work at the studio spans worldbuilding, 3D development and custom production tools. He enjoys exploring how things work and sharing what he learns through open-source projects, practical guides and reflections on the creative process.

Garrett Kinder

Garrett is a Perth-based 3D artist and co-founder of Open Studios. Combining his passion for art and gaming, he creates 3D assets to help other developers bring their ideas to life.

He produces assets under his label Obsidian Arts and works freelance alongside his husband Paul. Alongside developing his own asset collections, he welcomes enquiries for custom models and artwork for games and other creative projects.

Our values

Open Studios acknowledges the Nyoongar Whadjuk people - traditional custodians of this land. We wish to acknowledge the strength of their continuing culture and pay our respects to Elders past and present.

Free and Open Source software is as much a way of thinking as it is a licensing scheme, as such we strive to be accountable, transparent, informative and useful to others in all business interactions and activities.

We embrace differences, cherishing the rich tapestry of backgrounds, experiences, and identities that each person brings. It is important to us that working relationships are not only productive but also built on a foundation of mutual respect and appreciation.
