Our process

We follow an iterative approach to project delivery, adapting as we go to ensure resources are prioritised according to our needs and budget.

Project Definition

Using best practices to create a project plan that is robust and holds up in different scenarios, maximising budget efficiency and minimising risks.

Prototype Iteration

We follow a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) approach which allows us to be more efficient, collaborative and adaptive for challenges along the way.

Stakeholder Review

We ensure projects stay on track to successful completion by providing regular progress updates, check-ins and implementing notes and feedback.

Refinement

As multiple cycles of iteration are completed, the functionality, usability and overall quality of the project matures until it's ready for launch.

Ongoing Support

We are committed to our studio projects and to produce them openly. If you want to lodge a ticket you may do so on Github, or reach out on LinkedIn.

Our values

Open Studios acknowledges the Nyoongar Whadjuk people - traditional custodians of this land. We wish to acknowledge the strength of their continuing culture and pay our respects to Elders past and present.

Free and Open Source software is as much a way of thinking as it is a licensing scheme, as such we strive to be accountable, transparent, informative and useful to others in all business interactions and activities.

We embrace differences, cherishing the rich tapestry of backgrounds, experiences, and identities that each person brings. It is important to us that working relationships are not only productive but also built on a foundation of mutual respect and appreciation.
